3D models used by the "Readymade" figures (src/components/readymade/), the found-object mobile
(src/components/objets/) and the opening sequence (src/components/opening/).

Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Textures were downscaled
for the web; some maps were removed.

The halftone prints in public/images/press/ are renders of these same CC0 models, made with the
site's own print pass (src/components/readymade/print.ts) and used as the scrolling background wall.
